Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Jérémie Colin ("we") publishes the GuessTime app (the "App") on iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and the choices you have.

Controller and contact

Jérémie Colin, France. Contact us through the "Contact developer" link on the App Store or Google Play listing of GuessTime.

No account, no personal details

The App has no user account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Teams, players and custom themes you create stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads, through Google's consent form. On iOS, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking. How Google uses data for ads.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) records anonymous usage events, such as screens opened or games played, with device model and OS version, to improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects crash reports (device model, OS version, stack trace) to fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) downloads settings for the App using an installation identifier.
  • RevenueCat handles in-app purchases (theme packs, ad removal). It receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase history from the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details: Apple and Google process payments. RevenueCat privacy policy.

See the Google privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Why we use data (legal bases)

  • To provide the App and your purchases: performance of a contract.
  • Analytics, crash reports and remote settings: our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it.
  • Personalized ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell your personal data.

Children

GuessTime is a family party game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and transfers

Third-party providers keep data for the periods set in their own policies (for example, Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months). Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Union, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights and choices

  • You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent by contacting us.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can file a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
